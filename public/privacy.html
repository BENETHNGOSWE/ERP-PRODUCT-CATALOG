Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Last Updated: October 09, 2026 • Operated by KODA TECHNOLOGIES (Reg No: 618358)

1. Overview

Achete (accessible at https://achete.me) is a digital commerce and storefront platform operated by KODA TECHNOLOGIES. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when using our platform and WhatsApp integration services.

2. Information We Collect

When you place an order or interact with our merchant stores, we collect essential transaction details:

  • Customer contact information (name, phone number, delivery address).
  • Order details (items purchased, quantities, prices, timestamp).
  • Communication metadata for transactional order fulfillment via WhatsApp.

3. WhatsApp Cloud API & Communications

Achete utilizes official Meta WhatsApp Cloud API infrastructure to deliver real-time order receipts, status updates, and customer-merchant fulfillment communications. We do not sell or share customer contact information with third-party advertisers.

4. Data Protection & Security

We implement industry-standard encryption, role-based access control, and secure cloud infrastructure to protect all store and customer data from unauthorized access or disclosure.

5. User Data Deletion Instructions

In accordance with Meta Platform policies, users and merchants may request the complete deletion of their account, customer records, and order history at any time by contacting our data protection officer at privacy@kodatechnologies.co.tz or info@kodafreight.com. Upon receiving your request, all associated personal records will be permanently removed within 30 days.
